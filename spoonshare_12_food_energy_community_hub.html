Clean Induction Cooking vs. Fossil Gas

Conventional gas stoves lose up to 60% of their heat to the surrounding kitchen air and emit indoor pollutants. Electromagnetic induction transfers 85–90% of energy directly into the cookware—aligning with SDG 12.c's shift away from fossil fuels.
